## App Review information

Sign-in required: **no**. Contact: Anton Bernt, hello@lambking.store, phone ✏️.

Notes (English, pasted into „Notizen“):

> LambKing Stories is a free Bible story app for children in the Kids category. No account or login is needed; all stories are loaded from our own server and cached on the device. There are no ads, no third-party SDKs and no in-app purchases, and the iOS version contains no donation links.
>
> Parental gate: every link that leaves the app first shows a parental gate that asks the user to type the result of a multiplication (for example 7 × 8); "Cancel" keeps the child in the app. To see it, open the home screen, swipe the welcome banner to the second slide ("Discover our book world") and tap "Discover", which would open our book shop website lambking.store.
>
> Privacy: the privacy policy is in the app under "My Area" → "Privacy & Legal notice" (bottom of the page). To count daily active devices, the app sends a random, app-generated install ID to our own server with each request. It is not derived from device data, not linked to a person, not shared with anyone and not used for tracking; the server keeps one entry per ID and day and deletes entries after 13 months.
>
> The app supports iPhone and iPad in portrait and landscape.
